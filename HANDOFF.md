## What changed this session

Fixed a unit-mismatch bug in `deflated_alpha_ratio` (the Deflated Alpha Ratio,
multiple-testing correction for benchmark alpha) in
`skills/quantitative-backtesting/scripts/walk_forward.py`.

Root cause: `sr = alpha / strat_vol_ann` is annualized-scale, and the
cross-sectional `trial_sr` was also annualized, but the z-formula
`(sr - sr*) * sqrt(T-1) / denom` is derived for PER-PERIOD Sharpe quantities,
and the i.i.d.-null fallback `sr* = sqrt(2*ln(N)/T)` is period-scale. When
`trial_alphas` was not provided (fallback path), annualized `sr` was compared
against period-scale `sr*`, inflating `z` by ~sqrt(ann_factor) and biasing DAR
toward 1.0 (overstated significance). Empirically: positive-alpha fallback gave
DAR=1.0000 (z=22.19) pre-fix vs the correct DAR=0.8144 (z=0.89) post-fix.

Fix (walk_forward.py):
- Rescale `sr` and cross-sectional `trial_sr` to per-period units
  (`/ np.sqrt(ann_factor)`), matching the z-formula's derivation and the
  period-scale fallback `sr*`.
- Scale `expected_max_alpha` by `sqrt(ann_factor)` to preserve the documented
  annualized-alpha contract (unchanged for the cross-sectional path; corrected
  for the fallback path).

New regression test:
- `test_deflated_alpha_ratio_fallback_units_are_period_consistent` in
  `tests/test_walk_forward.py`: verifies the fallback (trial_alphas=None) path
  matches an independent per-period recomputation (z within 1e-9) and does not
  saturate at DAR=1.0. Registered in `TEST_REGISTRY` (required by the
  `test_main_registers_every_module_level_test` audit).

Test maintenance:
- `test_deflated_alpha_ratio_frequency_invariant`: raised `alpha_annual` 0.15 ->
  0.40 so DAR > 0.99 genuinely holds under correct per-period units (the old
  threshold only passed because the bug inflated `sr` by sqrt(ann_factor)).
  Frequency-invariance assertion (`abs(dar_daily - dar_monthly) < 0.05`)
  preserved and now passes with correct units.

Side effect: `examples/example_output/multiple_benchmarks_metrics.json`
regenerated by the example-run test with corrected (non-overstated) DAR and
p-value values.

No production methodology was removed; backward-compatible (degenerate case still
emits DAR=0, p_value=1, z_score=None, expected_max_alpha≈0).
